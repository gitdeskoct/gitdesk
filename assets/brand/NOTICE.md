# gitdesk logo

The gitdesk logo, app icon and tray icon are the octopus from Google's Noto Emoji
(https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji, file `2D/svg/emoji_u1f419.svg`), Copyright 2013 Google LLC.

gitdesk changed its colours from reds to blues (`assets/logo.svg` lists the new values). The
noto-emoji repository licenses its contents under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1; the full
text is in `noto-emoji-LICENSE.txt` beside this file, and it ships with every gitdesk build in
`resources/licenses`. The recoloured drawing stays under that licence.
